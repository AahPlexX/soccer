# Independent audit, November 2026: tasks and status

Two reviewers with no knowledge of the project were each fenced to one half of it and asked to criticise it as a user:

- **Coach board audit** (no access to Game Mode). Two passes. Pass 1 was **static only**: its attempt to run the app in a browser was blocked by a tool hook, so none of its 20 findings were verified at runtime by the reviewer; the maintainer verified a subset by measurement (see "Measured" below). Pass 2 reviewed 20 screenshots of the real board (laptop 1366x768, tablet 1024x768, phone 390x844) plus the source: 50 findings.
- **Game Mode audit** (no access to the coach board). It played the game in a real browser at several sizes and simulated whole matches in node: 26 findings, mostly verified by the reviewer.

Every finding is below, merged where both coach passes said the same thing, with its status. **Keep this file current**: tick a box in the same commit as the fix. `[x]` fixed, `[~]` partly fixed, `[ ]` open, `[-]` checked and found not to be true (reason given).

## Measured by the maintainer (coach board, real browser, no repo changes)

- No horizontal scroll, no clipped controls and no console errors at 1366x768, 1024x768, 768x1024 or 390x844.
- Every toolbar button is at least 44 px. Only the player tokens (about 38x51 px on a laptop, 20x27 on a phone) and the ball (22 px on a laptop, 11 px on a phone) are smaller than 44 px; the token hit circles are larger than the drawn token.
- The only text under 12 px is one phone label (11 px).
- Reset and import both record an undo step. The update dialog has a dismiss button.

## Game Mode

### High
- [ ] G-01 Taps and shots are dropped on 120/144/240 Hz screens: the input edge events are cleared when a frame runs no fixed step.
- [ ] G-02 A gamepad cannot operate any menu or resume from pause.
- [ ] G-03 Corners, goal kicks and free kicks resume after 1 s with the taker already holding the ball; only throw-ins wait for the player.
- [ ] G-04 Standing still with the ball is an exploit: a "do nothing" player kept the ball 214 to 232 of 240 s on every level; the AI almost never tackles a stationary carrier and Hard barely threatens.

### Medium
- [ ] G-05 Scoring is very hard and the keeper saves almost everything (even on Easy).
- [ ] G-06 Hard dominates a weak human and there is no gentler level for young children.
- [ ] G-07 Control jumps to another player about once a second; a human cannot make an off-ball run while the team has the ball.
- [ ] G-08 The corner taker sits under the HUD buttons; the camera does not frame restarts.
- [ ] G-09 Fouls are crude (any slide within 0.75 m of an opponent, no warning, no cooldown).
- [ ] G-10 Esc is inconsistent (leaves the whole game from the title's submenus; resumes the match from the pause submenus; does nothing at full time).
- [ ] G-11 Restart match and Exit to coach have no confirmation.
- [ ] G-12 The player is not told which team they are or which way they attack (HOME/AWAY with no colour key).
- [ ] G-13 A very small screen (568x320) is crowded and the title menu is clipped.
- [ ] G-14 The tips card sits over the pitch while the match runs.
- [ ] G-15 The pass preview line runs under the Switch button.
- [~] G-16 The ball is small next to the players. It was enlarged in 1.2.3 (0.7 m per meter of scale); the reviewer measured about 25 px against 80 to 90 px players, still small.
- [ ] G-17 Header loops: 94 headers in one half; every header is redirected along the header's own heading with no cooldown.

### Low
- [ ] G-18 The REPLAY banner sits in the middle of the action and there is no skip hint.
- [ ] G-19 The goal net looks like grey boxes; posts and crossbar are unclear.
- [ ] G-20 A large dark band below the touchline.
- [ ] G-21 HUD jargon (#4 RD, #7 ST); pad button names (A/B/X/Y) assume one pad layout.
- [ ] G-22 Restart prompts are inconsistent and the throw-in's 9 s auto-throw has no countdown.
- [ ] G-23 Half-time is a 3 s banner with no score card; the docs mention a golden goal that does not exist; the docs mention a "Start from my board" button that is not on the title screen.
- [ ] G-24 Difficulty and half length can only be changed on the title screen.
- [ ] G-25 On a portrait phone the rotate overlay covers the title menu.
- [ ] G-26 Teams differ by blue vs red only (no colour-blind option); the scoreboard has aria-live off and the canvas has no text alternative.
