# Independent audit, November 2026: tasks and status

Two reviewers with no knowledge of the project were each fenced to one half of it and asked to criticise it as a user:

- **Coach board audit** (no access to Game Mode). Two passes. Pass 1 was **static only**: its attempt to run the app in a browser was blocked by a tool hook, so none of its 20 findings were verified at runtime by the reviewer; the maintainer verified a subset by measurement (see "Measured" below). Pass 2 reviewed 20 screenshots of the real board (laptop 1366x768, tablet 1024x768, phone 390x844) plus the source: 50 findings.
- **Game Mode audit** (no access to the coach board). It played the game in a real browser at several sizes and simulated whole matches in node: 26 findings, mostly verified by the reviewer.

Every finding is below, merged where both coach passes said the same thing, with its status. **Keep this file current**: tick a box in the same commit as the fix. `[x]` fixed, `[~]` partly fixed, `[ ]` open, `[-]` checked and found not to be true (reason given).

## Measured by the maintainer (coach board, real browser, no repo changes)

- No horizontal scroll, no clipped controls and no console errors at 1366x768, 1024x768, 768x1024 or 390x844.
- Every toolbar button is at least 44 px. Only the player tokens (about 38x51 px on a laptop, 20x27 on a phone) and the ball (22 px on a laptop, 11 px on a phone) are smaller than 44 px; the token hit circles are larger than the drawn token.
- The only text under 12 px is one phone label (11 px).
- Reset and import both record an undo step. The update dialog has a dismiss button.

## Game Mode

### High
- [x] G-01 Taps and shots are dropped on 120/144/240 Hz screens: the input edge events are cleared when a frame runs no fixed step. **Fixed in 1.2.6: taps from a frame that ran no step are kept for the next one (`mergeFrames`).**
- [x] G-02 A gamepad cannot operate any menu or resume from pause. **Fixed in 1.2.6: D-pad or stick up/down moves the focus, A presses, B goes back, Start resumes from pause.**
- [x] G-03 Corners, goal kicks and free kicks resume after 1 s with the taker already holding the ball; only throw-ins wait for the player. **Fixed in 1.2.6: all restarts wait for the human to aim and kick with pass or shoot (computer after 1.6 s, human timeout 9 s).**
- [x] G-04 Standing still with the ball is an exploit: a "do nothing" player kept the ball 214 to 232 of 240 s on every level; the AI almost never tackles a stationary carrier and Hard barely threatens. **Fixed in 1.2.6: the pressing player closes to 0.7 m and tackles a slow or stationary carrier (tackles on an idle player went from 0 to 1 a match to 2 to 6); possession was a poor measure because the keeper holding it counts for the human side.**

### Medium
- [ ] G-05 Scoring is very hard and the keeper saves almost everything (even on Easy).
- [x] G-06 Hard dominates a weak human and there is no gentler level for young children. **Added a Kids level (listed first on the title screen), tested to be steadily weaker than Easy.**
- [ ] G-07 Control jumps to another player about once a second; a human cannot make an off-ball run while the team has the ball.
- [x] G-08 The corner taker sits under the HUD buttons; the camera does not frame restarts. **Fixed in 1.2.6: at a restart the camera frames the spot a little further into the pitch.**
- [ ] G-09 Fouls are crude (any slide within 0.75 m of an opponent, no warning, no cooldown).
- [x] G-10 Esc is inconsistent (leaves the whole game from the title's submenus; resumes the match from the pause submenus; does nothing at full time). **Fixed in 1.2.6: Esc and the gamepad B always go one level back (title: leave; pause: resume).**
- [x] G-11 Restart match and Exit to coach have no confirmation. **Fixed in 1.2.6: Restart match and Exit to coach in the pause menu need a second press within 3 s.**
- [x] G-12 The player is not told which team they are or which way they attack (HOME/AWAY with no colour key). **Fixed in 1.2.6: the scoreboard says YOU or CPU with an arrow for the direction each side attacks.**
- [ ] G-13 A very small screen (568x320) is crowded and the title menu is clipped.
- [x] G-14 The tips card sits over the pitch while the match runs. **Fixed in 1.2.6: the match waits while the first-run tips card is up (the card says so).**
- [ ] G-15 The pass preview line runs under the Switch button.
- [~] G-16 The ball is small next to the players. It was enlarged in 1.2.3 (0.7 m per meter of scale); the reviewer measured about 25 px against 80 to 90 px players, still small.
- [x] G-17 Header loops: 94 headers in one half; every header is redirected along the header's own heading with no cooldown. **Fixed in 1.2.6: one header per 0.7 s across the match.**

### Low
- [~] G-18 The REPLAY banner sits in the middle of the action and there is no skip hint. **1.2.6: the banner moved to a corner and says to tap anything to skip; whether the replay shows the goal rather than the celebration is not checked.**
- [ ] G-19 The goal net looks like grey boxes; posts and crossbar are unclear.
- [ ] G-20 A large dark band below the touchline.
- [~] G-21 HUD jargon (#4 RD, #7 ST); pad button names (A/B/X/Y) assume one pad layout. **1.2.6: the HUD uses role names (Striker, Defender); pad button names (A/B/X/Y) are still shown on the Controls screen.**
- [ ] G-22 Restart prompts are inconsistent and the throw-in's 9 s auto-throw has no countdown.
- [ ] G-23 Half-time is a 3 s banner with no score card; the docs mention a golden goal that does not exist; the docs mention a "Start from my board" button that is not on the title screen.
